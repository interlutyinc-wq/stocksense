## CANALE 1 — Cold Outreach Diretto (Priorità Massima)

### Target
Merchant Shopify USA con queste caratteristiche:
- 50-500 prodotti nel catalogo
- Store attivo da almeno 6 mesi
- Vendor field compilato (= hanno fornitori strutturati)
- Categorie: elettronica, abbigliamento, casa, sport, beauty

### Come trovare i merchant
Shopify ha store pubblici. Cerca su Google:
```
site:myshopify.com "powered by shopify" inurl:collections
```
Oppure usa **Apollo.io** o **Hunter.io** per trovare email di ecommerce manager.

### Template email (mandare da interlutyinc@gmail.com)

**Subject:** Your Shopify inventory is costing you money — here's how to fix it

**Body:**
> Hi [Name],
>
> I noticed your store [store name] — you're selling [product category] and likely managing reorders manually.
>
> Most Shopify merchants lose 30%+ of revenue to stockouts because they react too late. I built StockSense to fix this — it's an AI agent that reads your live inventory, tells you exactly what to reorder and why, and sends the purchase order to your supplier automatically.
>
> Built it in 7 days. It's live now.
>
> Free to try: [link]
>
> Would love 15 minutes to show you — does this week work?
>
> Albion
> Founder, StockSense

### Volume target
- **Giorno 1-2:** 50 email (20 risposte attese, 3-5 demo)
- **Giorno 3-5:** 50 email followup
- **Conversione attesa:** 2-3 clienti paganti

- **Frame da usare nell'outreach:** "StockSense costa $49/mese. Un stockout costa 10x di più."
